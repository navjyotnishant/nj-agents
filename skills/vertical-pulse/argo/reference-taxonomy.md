# Vertical taxonomy

Ground the research in a real vertical rather than free text. Match the user's
input case-insensitively; on a match, adopt that vertical's sub-verticals as the
research scope.

| Vertical | Sub-verticals |
|---|---|
| **FinTech** | Enterprise Fintech Solutions · Lending & Finance · Payment Tech · Wealth & Crypto · Cross-Border Payments · Construction Finance · Crypto & Stablecoins |
| **Media & Advertising** | AdTech · Advertising & Marketing · Entertainment Production · Publishing · Video Streaming & OTT |
| **Software & Hi-Tech** | Greenfield Development · Experience Modernization · Technology Transformation · Platform Consolidation |
| **Travel & Hospitality** | Airlines · Car Rentals · Distribution · Hospitality · Events and Meetings |
| **Retail** | (research the vertical directly) |
| **Supply Chain & Logistics** | (research the vertical directly) |
| **Healthcare & Life Sciences** | (research the vertical directly) |

## A vertical outside this list

Fine. Research it directly and **note in the report that no sub-vertical scope was
applied**, so the reader knows the sweep was broader and shallower than usual.

**Never silently substitute a different vertical.** If the input does not match,
say so and research it as given rather than snapping it to the nearest known name.
